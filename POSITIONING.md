# Positioning

## What this project is

EconoSpec is an independent, open source conformance test suite for
econometric estimation. It was started by a student of the Department of
Econometrics at Bursa Uludag University, and the choice of estimator families
reflects that curriculum: statistics, econometrics and operations research.

## What this project is not

- It is **not** an official product of Bursa Uludag University.
- It is **not** an official product of the Faculty of Economics and
  Administrative Sciences or of the Department of Econometrics.
- It does **not** speak for any faculty member, department or institution.
- It does **not** use university logos, seals, colours or document templates.
- It does **not** contain student records, enrolment data, grades or any
  personal data. Every fixture is synthetic and generated by committed code.
- It does **not** access UNISIS, UKEY or any university system.

If the department ever grants written permission to describe the project
differently, that permission will be recorded here with its date, and only
then will the wording change.

## Why the boundary matters

A conformance suite makes claims about correctness. If those claims arrive
wearing an institutional badge they were never given, the badge does the
persuading instead of the evidence. The point of this repository is that the
evidence is runnable by anyone who disagrees with it.

## Naming

The repository name is `econospec`. The long form is
"Econometric Specification and Verification Lab". Neither form contains a
university name, and no release artefact is titled after one.

## Scope of claims

- Agreement between implementations is evidence that a computation is
  reproducible. It is not evidence that a model is correctly specified for any
  real world question.
- Nothing here is statistical, economic, legal or financial advice.
- Results are educational demonstrations of method, and every number is
  traceable to a committed fixture, a committed spec and a committed tolerance.
